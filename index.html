AI Picture Frame

Your photos, reimagined on your wall.

AI Picture Frame is a subscription app that turns a household photo frame into a rotating gallery of AI-stylized versions of your own photos.

How it works

Pair a tablet frame with the phone app, pick the photos and the art style you like, and the frame shows a fresh artwork on the schedule you choose.

Status

AI Picture Frame is in a small private beta. A public launch is planned.

Account emails

We send email only when you ask for it: a confirmation when you sign up and a link when you reset your password. We never send marketing email.
